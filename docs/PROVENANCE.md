# Provenance and attribution

## Historical context

Documented 2023-24 economic-history research workflow at the University of Tübingen under Prof. Jörg Baten.

## This public edition

New public reconstruction using synthetic data; institutional research records and unpublished findings excluded.

Arinjai Das supported data preparation, applied econometrics, specification comparisons and reporting in the original research setting. The public reconstruction demonstrates reusable analytical tooling and does not claim sole ownership of the collaborative empirical research.

## Scope of the evidence

The verification record concerns the public files and example described here. It does not establish a recovered historical trading record, a peer-reviewed publication, ownership of collaborators' work or independent reproduction of excluded archives. Synthetic examples are labelled at the source, metadata and results levels.

Recovered research materials, where included, are labelled separately from reconstructed examples. No original participant-level survey records or institutional microdata are distributed.
